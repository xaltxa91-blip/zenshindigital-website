Google explains the “Under re-review” ad status: why approved ads stop serving

Google has updated its policy documentation to explain the “Under re-review” status: previously approved ads can be re-reviewed when the ad, landing page or account is flagged. The ads show “Eligible (limited)” and don't serve, typically for up to 48 hours.

Key facts

  • Previously approved ads or assets can be re-reviewed if they, their landing page or the associated account get flagged.
  • During a re-review, the ad shows as “Eligible (limited)” and does not serve.
  • Re-reviews typically conclude within 48 hours, though this is not guaranteed.
  • The re-review itself can't be appealed, because it is not a policy decision. A resulting disapproval or suspension can be.

Google has updated its policy documentation to explain the “Under re-review” ad status, according to PPC News Feed, which reported the change on November 21, 2026. The update was spotted by Hana Kobzová. The status itself is not new: advertisers first spotted it in their accounts in August 2026. What's new is Google's official explanation.

What triggers a re-review

Ads and assets that were already approved can be reviewed again. According to the updated documentation, a re-review can happen when any of these gets flagged:

  • the ad or asset itself
  • its destination (the landing page)
  • the associated account

What happens during the re-review

QuestionAnswer
Which status is shown?“Eligible (limited)”
Does the ad serve?No. Ads under re-review do not serve.
How long does it take?Re-reviews typically conclude within 48 hours. This is typical, not guaranteed.
Can I appeal?Not the re-review itself, because it is not a policy decision. If it ends in a disapproval or suspension, those decisions can be appealed.

Why it matters this week

Black Friday falls on November 27, 2026. Because an ad under re-review does not serve while it shows “Eligible (limited)”, an approved ad can stop delivering without being disapproved, and there is no appeal to speed it up.

How to check your ads

  1. In Google Ads, open Campaigns → Ads (or Assets) and look at the Status column.
  2. Hover over an “Eligible (limited)” status to see the reason: it can mean a re-review or a policy restriction, such as location or age limits.
  3. If an ad or asset is under re-review, there is no appeal for the re-review itself. If it ends in a disapproval, you can fix the ad or appeal the decision from the Policy manager.

FAQ

What does “Under re-review” mean in Google Ads?

A previously approved ad or asset is being reviewed again because the ad, its landing page or the associated account was flagged.

Does an ad under re-review still serve?

No. During the re-review the ad shows “Eligible (limited)” and does not serve.

How long does a Google Ads re-review take?

Re-reviews typically conclude within 48 hours, but this is not guaranteed.

Can I appeal a re-review?

No. The re-review is not a policy decision, so it can't be appealed. If it ends in a disapproval or suspension, you can appeal that decision.
