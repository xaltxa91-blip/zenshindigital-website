Amazon is running Black Friday ads in ChatGPT

According to Digiday, Amazon is running a new round of Black Friday ads in ChatGPT in the US, in ChatGPT's standard ad format. It's unclear whether the ads were bought directly from OpenAI or through Amazon DSP.

Key facts

  • Digiday reports that Amazon is running a new round of Black Friday ads in ChatGPT in the US.
  • The ads use ChatGPT's standard format, separate from the conversation, with a favicon, headline, description and “Learn more” button.
  • It's unclear whether Amazon bought them directly from OpenAI or through Amazon DSP.
  • Amazon and OpenAI declined to comment.

Amazon is running a new round of Black Friday ads in ChatGPT in the US, according to Digiday, which reported on November 24, 2026 that it had seen the ads. Neither company has confirmed it: Amazon and OpenAI both declined to comment on Digiday's article.

What the ads look like

  • Format: the same format as typical ChatGPT ads, separate from the user conversation, with a favicon, headline, description and a “Learn more” call to action.
  • Content: the ads promote Amazon Black Friday deals.
  • Destination: they direct users to Amazon to complete their purchases.
  • Market: the US.

How Amazon bought the ads is unclear

Digiday says it is unclear whether Amazon bought its ChatGPT ads directly from OpenAI or through its own Amazon DSP.

In September, Amazon Ads announced a partnership that lets advertisers buy ChatGPT inventory through Amazon DSP. According to Digiday, the integration initially gives select US advertisers access to ChatGPT's ad inventory.

ChatGPT's shopping results

Separately from the ads, Digiday reports that ChatGPT's shopping experience links to Amazon search results, drawing on third-party deal-tracking websites to identify products, discounts and up-to-date pricing.
