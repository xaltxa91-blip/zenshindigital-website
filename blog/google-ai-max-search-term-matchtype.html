Google Ads: new {search_term_matchtype} parameter shows AI Max expansion clicks

A new ValueTrack parameter, {search_term_matchtype}, shows when a click on an exact-match keyword actually came from an AI Max expansion.

Google Ads has documented a new ValueTrack parameter, {search_term_matchtype}. It shows how the search term actually matched, including clicks that came from AI Max expansions.

Same click, two parameters

When AI Max expands an exact-match keyword:

  • {matchtype} returns e, so it looks like an exact match.
  • {search_term_matchtype} returns a, an AI Max expansion.

{search_term_matchtype} values

  • e: exact
  • p: phrase
  • b: broad
  • a: AI Max expansions and keywordless

Google's example tracking template

{lpurl}?keyword={keyword}&matchtype={matchtype}&search_term_matchtype={search_term_matchtype}
