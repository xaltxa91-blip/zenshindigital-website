ChatGPT Ads now reports Reach & Frequency, plus 7 more audience metrics

OpenAI added Reach, Frequency and 7 more audience metrics to ChatGPT Ads Manager and the Ads API. The same update adds a default “Learn more” link to single-image ads, Business Agents outside the US and an option to hide sale prices.

Key facts

  • ChatGPT Ads Manager and the Ads API now report Reach and Frequency, plus 7 related audience metrics.
  • Reach estimates unique people who saw an ad at least once. Frequency is total impressions divided by Reach.
  • Single-image ads now show a “Learn more” link by default.
  • Business Agents are open to eligible businesses in supported markets outside the US.

OpenAI sent ChatGPT advertisers a “ChatGPT Ads Product Update” email on November 25, 2026, first reported by Search Engine Roundtable. The biggest change: Reach and Frequency reporting in Ads Manager and the Ads API.

Reach and Frequency

  • Reach: for the selected date range, estimates how many unique people saw your ads at least once.
  • Frequency: estimates how many times those people saw your ads on average, calculated as total impressions divided by Reach for the same period.

OpenAI named these metrics in the email but did not define them.

How to add the metrics in Ads Manager

  1. Open your ChatGPT Ads Manager report and click Edit columns.
  2. Select the metrics under Ad Viewers.
  3. Click Save changes.

The metrics are also available in the Ads API.

Other changes in the update

  • “Learn more” link: single-image ads now show a “Learn more” link below the description by default. It opens the landing page already set in Ads Manager, so no extra setup is needed.
  • Business Agents outside the US: eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in supported non-US markets can create and publish a Business Agent in the ChatGPT Business Hub, and add it to a campaign as a Sponsored Business Agent. OpenAI did not name the markets.
  • Hide sale prices: for product-feed ad groups, advertisers can hide sale prices in Ads Manager, so the regular price shows again after a promotion ends.
  • Policy Center details: review messages for rejected ads now show more context on which policy applies. OpenAI said its ad approval guidelines have not changed.

FAQ

Does ChatGPT Ads report reach and frequency?

Yes. Since OpenAI's November 25, 2026 update, Ads Manager and the Ads API report Reach and Frequency, plus 7 related audience metrics.

How is Frequency calculated in ChatGPT Ads?

Frequency is total impressions divided by Reach for the same reporting period.

How do I add Reach and Frequency columns in ChatGPT Ads Manager?

Click Edit columns, select the metrics under Ad Viewers, then click Save changes.

Do single-image ChatGPT ads have a call-to-action?

Yes. Single-image ads now show a “Learn more” link by default, which opens the landing page set in Ads Manager.
