Amazon Ads bans claims like “Best price” and “Unbeatable price” in ads

Amazon Ads updated its Pricing and promotions policy on November 15, 2026. Price superlatives like “Best price”, “Unbeatable price” and “Wholesale price” are not allowed on Amazon's own placements. Here are the new rules by placement.

Key facts

  • Amazon Ads updated its “Pricing and promotions” policy page on November 15, 2026.
  • Price superlatives and unprovable price claims, like “Best price” or “Unbeatable price”, are not allowed on Amazon retail or non-retail placements.
  • On Amazon retail, numerical prices or savings are only allowed when Amazon renders them dynamically in the ad.
  • The page gives no effective date or transition period.

Amazon Ads updated its “Pricing and promotions” policy page on November 15, 2026. The page is part of the Policies and guidelines section of the Amazon Ads Support Center, and it sorts the rules by where the ad runs (supply type) instead of by ad product. It gives no effective date, changelog or transition period.

Several other Amazon Ads policy pages show the same update date: Claims and substantiation, Real estate, News and journalism, Animal related products and services, and Gambling and games.

Price claims that are no longer allowed

The policy prohibits price superlatives and competitive, misleading or unprovable price claims on Amazon retail and non-retail placements. Amazon lists these as examples (the list is not complete):

  • “Best price” or “Top price”
  • “Unbeatable price”
  • “Competitive price”
  • “Best possible price”
  • “Introductory price”
  • “Wholesale price”
  • “Never before seen price”
  • “Price advantage”

Rules for Amazon retail placements

Amazon retail covers Amazon.com retail placements and Whole Foods Market digital placements. Here the policy states:

  • Prices and savings: “Numerical prices or savings are only permitted when rendered dynamically in the ad by Amazon.” Prices or savings written into the creative itself are not allowed.
  • Landing page match: pricing, savings and promotional claims must match the landing page for the full campaign.
  • Multi-product pages and Brand Stores: when an ad links to a page with several products, at least 10% of the products (or the share required in that country) must carry the maximum saving claimed in the ad.
  • Deal teasing is not allowed.
  • Urgency language that pressures a decision, such as “buy now or regret later”, is not allowed.

Rules for non-retail Amazon placements

Non-retail supply covers IMDb, Fire TV and Streaming TV. Here, claims must be available for the full campaign and must disclose material conditions, such as exclusions, delivery costs, subscriptions or bundles. The ban on price superlatives and unprovable claims also applies.

Rules for the open internet

For ads on third-party publisher sites and apps, the advertiser is responsible for accuracy, for complying with applicable laws, and for meeting the publisher's own requirements. The page gives no list of banned wording for this supply type.

How to check your Amazon ads against the new rules

  1. Search your headlines, custom images and video copy for the phrases listed above.
  2. For ads on Amazon retail, remove prices or savings written into the creative. Only prices Amazon shows dynamically are allowed.
  3. Check that every price, saving or promotion in the ad matches the landing page for the whole campaign.
  4. For ads that link to a Brand Store or multi-product page, check that at least 10% of the products carry the maximum saving you claim.
  5. For IMDb, Fire TV and Streaming TV ads, add any material conditions, such as delivery costs or subscriptions.

FAQ

When did Amazon Ads update its pricing policy?

The Pricing and promotions policy page shows an update date of November 15, 2026. Amazon has not given a separate effective date or transition period.

Can I write prices in my Amazon ad creative?

On Amazon retail placements, numerical prices or savings are only allowed when Amazon renders them dynamically in the ad. Prices written into the creative are not allowed there.

Which placements does the ban on “Best price” claims cover?

Amazon retail (Amazon.com and Whole Foods Market digital) and non-retail Amazon supply (IMDb, Fire TV and Streaming TV).

Is the list of banned phrases complete?

No. Amazon gives the phrases as examples. Any price superlative or competitive, misleading or unprovable price claim is covered.
