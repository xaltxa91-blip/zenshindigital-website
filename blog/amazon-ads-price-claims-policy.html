Amazon Ads bans claims like “Unbeatable price” in ad copy

Amazon Ads updated its advertising copy policy on November 15, 2026. Price claims such as “Best price” or “Wholesale price” are no longer allowed on Amazon's own placements.

Amazon Ads updated its advertising copy policy on November 15, 2026. The changes apply to ads on Amazon's own placements.

Example phrases now banned in ads

  • Best / Top price
  • Unbeatable price
  • Competitive price
  • Wholesale price
  • Never before seen price

Also in the update

  • On Amazon retail, prices or savings can only appear when they are rendered by Amazon, not written in the creative.
  • Health, environmental, origin and comparison claims are allowed with dated evidence.
  • The policy covers Amazon retail, IMDb, Fire TV and Streaming TV.
  • No transition period has been announced.
