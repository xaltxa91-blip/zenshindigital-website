Meta Ads Manager: up to 5 existing posts in one ad

Some Meta Ads Manager accounts can now select up to 5 published posts in a single ad. Meta has not announced it yet.

Some Meta Ads Manager accounts now show a new option: under “Use existing posts”, advertisers can select up to 5 published posts in one ad. I found it in my own Ads Manager account.

What's new

  • Select up to 5 published posts in a single ad, under “Use existing posts”.
  • Meta shows each person the post they're most likely to engage with.
  • Previously, an ad could use only 1 existing post.

Still unknown

  • Meta has not published an official announcement or rollout details yet.

Sources

  • Screenshot from my Meta Ads Manager account
