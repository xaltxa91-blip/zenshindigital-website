Meta Ads Manager now lets you add up to 5 existing posts in one ad

Meta Ads Manager is showing a new option to add up to 5 published posts to a single ad, with Meta showing each person the post they're most likely to engage with. Here's what is confirmed and what is still unknown.

Key facts

  • Under “Use existing posts”, advertisers can now select up to 5 published posts in one ad.
  • Meta shows each person the post it predicts they're most likely to engage with.
  • Before, this setup allowed 1 existing post per ad.
  • Meta has not published an announcement, help page or rollout details yet.

Meta Ads Manager is showing a new option for ads built from existing posts: instead of one published post per ad, advertisers can now select up to 5 posts in a single ad. The option was shared on LinkedIn by Bram Van der Hallen (Edge.be) on November 17, 2026, and reported by PPC Land the same day. We can also see it in our own Meta Ads Manager account, as the screenshot above shows.

Meta has not announced the change or documented it in its Business Help Center yet, so some details are still unknown. This article covers what can be confirmed from Ads Manager itself.

Where to find the new option in Ads Manager

  1. Create or edit an ad and go to the ad level.
  2. Under Ad setup, choose “Use existing posts”.
  3. In the Ad creative section, the Posts field now shows a counter, for example “0 of 5 posts selected”.
  4. Click “Select posts” to pick up to 5 published posts, or enter a post ID.

In the accounts where it has appeared, Ads Manager shows this notice: “Now you can select up to 5 posts, and we'll show each person the one they're most likely to engage with.”

How it works

All selected posts sit inside one ad. For each person who sees the ad, Meta chooses the post it predicts that person is most likely to engage with. Until now, promoting 5 existing posts under the same setup required 5 separate ads.

Ads that use existing posts are different from new ad creatives: they run the original organic post, so the reactions, comments and shares the post already has stay visible on the ad.

What is still unknown

Because Meta has not published documentation, these points are not confirmed:

  • Rollout: which countries and accounts have the option, and when it will reach everyone.
  • Eligibility: which campaign objectives and placements support it.
  • Reporting: whether results can be broken down per post.
  • Selection signal: Meta describes the choice only as the post a person is “most likely to engage with”. It's not stated whether this follows the ad set's optimisation goal, such as traffic, leads or sales.
  • Ad limits: whether an ad with 5 posts counts as 1 ad towards a Page's ad limit.
  • API: whether the option is available in the Marketing API.

Related Meta features

The new option sits next to other Meta formats that test several assets inside one ad:

  • Flexible format ads let advertisers add up to 10 images and videos to one ad, and Meta chooses which to show.
  • Advantage+ creative enhancements appear in the same Ad creative section of Ads Manager.

The difference: flexible format ads use new media files, while this option uses posts that are already published on the Page or Instagram account.

FAQ

How many existing posts can I add to one Meta ad?

Up to 5, under “Use existing posts” in the Ad creative section of Ads Manager. Previously the limit was 1 post per ad.

How does Meta choose which post to show?

Meta shows each person the post it predicts they're most likely to engage with. Meta has not published more detail on the selection.

Is the option available in all accounts?

Not confirmed. Meta has not announced a rollout, so it may only appear in some accounts for now.

Can I see results per post?

Not confirmed. Meta has not documented per-post reporting for this option yet.
