FAQ

Do I need to sell on Amazon to advertise?

For Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands, yes: you need to be a seller or vendor on Amazon. Amazon DSP and some other formats are also available to brands that don't sell on Amazon.

What is the difference between ACoS and TACoS?

ACoS is ad spend divided by ad-attributed sales. TACoS is ad spend divided by total sales, including organic sales.

Can I write prices in my Amazon ads?

On Amazon retail placements, numerical prices or savings are only allowed when Amazon renders them dynamically in the ad. See our article on Amazon's pricing policy update.
