FAQ

Where do Microsoft Ads appear?

On Bing and the Microsoft Search Network, which includes partner sites such as Yahoo and AOL, and in Microsoft Copilot.

Can I import my Google Ads campaigns?

Yes. Microsoft Advertising has a Google Ads import tool that copies campaigns, ad groups, keywords and ads.

What is UET?

UET (Universal Event Tracking) is Microsoft Advertising's tag for tracking conversions and building remarketing audiences.
