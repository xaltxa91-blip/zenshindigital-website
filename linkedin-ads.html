FAQ

Who can I target on LinkedIn?

LinkedIn lets you target by professional attributes such as job title, job function, seniority, company, company size and industry, as well as by your own matched audiences.

What are Thought Leader Ads?

Thought Leader Ads let a company promote posts published by its employees or other members, from their personal profiles.

What are Lead Gen Forms?

Lead Gen Forms are forms pre-filled with a member's LinkedIn profile data, so people can submit their details without leaving LinkedIn.
