FAQ

Is there a minimum budget for Google Ads?

Google Ads has no minimum spend. You set a daily budget per campaign and can change or pause it at any time.

What is AI Max for Search?

AI Max for Search is a set of features for Search campaigns that uses Google AI to match more search terms, customise ad text and choose the best landing page on your site.

What is Performance Max?

Performance Max is a goal-based campaign type that runs across all Google Ads channels from a single campaign, including Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps.
