FAQ

Where do Meta ads appear?

Meta ads can appear on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Threads and the Audience Network, depending on the placements selected.

Is there a minimum budget for Meta Ads?

There is no minimum commitment. You set a daily or lifetime budget per campaign or ad set, and can change or pause it at any time.

What is the Conversions API?

The Conversions API sends conversion events from your server directly to Meta. Used with the Meta Pixel, it makes measurement more reliable.
