FAQ

What are Sponsored Snaps?

Sponsored Snaps are ads delivered directly to the Chat inbox as a Snap, which people can open like a message.

What are Dynamic Product Ads on Snapchat?

Dynamic Product Ads are created automatically from your product catalog and show relevant products to each person.

How do I track conversions from Snapchat?

With the Snap Pixel on your website and the Snapchat Conversions API for server-side events.
