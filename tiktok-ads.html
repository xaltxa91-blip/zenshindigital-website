FAQ

What are Spark Ads?

Spark Ads let advertisers promote organic TikTok posts, from their own account or from creators who authorise it, as ads.

What is GMV Max?

GMV Max is TikTok's campaign type for TikTok Shop sellers, which optimises for gross merchandise value (total shop sales).

Is there a minimum budget for TikTok Ads?

TikTok sets minimum daily budgets at campaign and ad group level. The exact amounts depend on the currency and are shown in TikTok Ads Manager.
