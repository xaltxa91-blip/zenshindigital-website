FAQ

Where do Pinterest ads appear?

In the home feed, in search results and among related Pins.

What is Performance+?

Performance+ is Pinterest's suite of AI-powered campaign features that automate targeting, bidding and creative.

Do I need a product catalog?

Only for Shopping ads. Image and video ads can run without a catalog.
